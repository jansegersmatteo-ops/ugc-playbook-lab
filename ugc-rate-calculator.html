    How to use the result

    • Quote a range, sign a number. Use the low end for a first client or a package, the high end when the brand wants long or exclusive usage.
    • Put usage in writing. Where the video runs, for how long, and from whose account. Our usage rights guide has a plain-English clause.
    • Price extras separately. Extra hooks and raw footage are real extra work; listing them keeps your base rate clean.

    Where the numbers come from

    The default base rate of $100 is the median per-video offer in Pitchlo's report of 645 briefs (Dec 2025 – Oct 2026). The usage-rights percentages are Billo's typical add-ons. Hook and raw-footage defaults are placeholders: change them to your own prices. Rates vary a lot by niche, experience and country.
