UGC usage rights explained

When a brand buys a UGC video, it buys two things: the file, and the right to use it in certain places for a certain time. That second part, usage rights, is where much of the price difference comes from.

The four common levels

LevelWhat the brand may doTypical add-on (Billo)
OrganicPost on its own social accounts and websiteIncluded in base fee
Paid adsRun the video as an ad from its own account+15–30% of base per 30 days
Whitelisting / Spark AdsRun ads from your account handle+25–50% of base per 30 days
Long term or perpetualUse for 12 months or forever, everywhere+70–100% (12 months) or +150–300% (perpetual)

What to put in writing

  • Where the video may be used (organic, paid, which platforms).
  • For how long (30 days, 90 days, 12 months).
  • Whether ads run from your handle.
  • What happens after the period ends.

A plain-English clause

The brand may use the delivered videos organically on its own channels without time limit. Paid advertising use is licensed for [30/90] days from delivery for [fee]. Extensions are available at [fee] per 30 days.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check what applies in your country.
