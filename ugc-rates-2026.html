UGC rates in 2026: what brands actually pay

Search "UGC rates" and you'll find numbers from $30 to $3,000 per video. Both are real. The difference is experience, niche, and above all usage rights. Here is what the 2026 data says, with sources.

For a beginner, a realistic starting range is roughly $50 to $200 per video. That's not a promise; it's where most posted offers sit.

Why the same video can cost $100 or $900

  • Usage rights. Organic posting on the brand's own account is the base. Running your video as a paid ad costs more.
  • Niche. Tech, software and apps need someone who can explain a product, and tend to pay more. Beauty has huge demand but also huge competition.
  • Extras. Additional hooks, raw footage, rush delivery and extra revisions are usually priced separately.
  • Track record. Creators who can show ads that performed charge more.

Marketplaces vs. direct clients

Marketplaces pay less but bring you work, which is the hardest part when you're new. Direct clients usually pay more per video but you have to find and pitch them yourself. Most beginners do both: a marketplace for first jobs and testimonials, direct pitches for better rates.
