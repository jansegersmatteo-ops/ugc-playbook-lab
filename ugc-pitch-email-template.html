UGC pitch email templates that get replies

Most pitches fail because they're about the creator, not the brand. A good pitch is short, specific and offers one concrete idea.

Who to pitch

  • Brands already running creator-style ads (check public ad libraries).
  • Small and growing brands without an in-house content team.
  • Products you actually use.

The cold email template

Subject: UGC videos for [Brand]'s [product]

Hi [name],

I'm [your name], a UGC creator focused on [niche]. I noticed [Brand] is running ads for [product], and I think a [format, e.g. morning routine demo] could show [specific benefit] in a more natural way.

Here's my portfolio: [link]. For [Brand], I'd film [one-sentence idea].

Would you be open to a 3-video test package? I can send my rates.

[Your name]

The short DM version

Hi! I make UGC for [niche] brands. I had an idea for [product]: [one-sentence idea]. Portfolio: [link]. Open to a quick test?

The follow-up (once, after 5–7 days)

Hi [name], quick follow-up with a new angle for [product]: [new hook idea]. Happy to film a test video if it's a fit. Either way, thanks for reading!

Never send more than two messages without a reply.

Personalize every pitch with AI

Here is a brand's website text: [paste]. Here is my pitch template: [paste]. Fill in the template for this brand with one specific, believable video idea based on their product and customer. Max 120 words. No flattery, no exaggeration.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Long introductions about yourself.
  • The same message to everyone.
  • Promising results like "this will double your sales".
  • Using private contact details: stick to public business emails.
