How to build a UGC portfolio without clients

No brand will hire you without seeing your work, and you can't get work without a portfolio. The way out is spec videos: samples you make yourself with products you bought or own.

The 3 videos to make

  1. Problem–solution ad (20–30 sec): shows you can sell.
  2. Product demo or tutorial (30–45 sec): shows you can explain.
  3. Unboxing or honest review (15–30 sec): shows personality and trust.

Keep all three in your chosen niche.

Rules to stay honest

  • Use products you bought or own.
  • Label them as "sample" or "spec" work.
  • Never suggest a brand hired or endorsed you when it didn't.
  • Don't run them as paid ads or use brand logos as if you represent the brand.

Your portfolio page

  • One sentence: who you make videos for.
  • Your 3 videos, playable on the page.
  • What you offer: ad videos, demos, extra hooks, raw footage.
  • A contact email for UGC work.

Leave prices off the page and send your rate card on request.

Quality checklist before you publish

  • Hook in the first 3 seconds
  • Product clearly visible and correctly named
  • Clear sound, correct captions
  • No other brands in view
